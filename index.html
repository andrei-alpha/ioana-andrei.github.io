FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers about the Blue shuttle from Pipera Metro, dress code, and wedding schedule.

It's effortless! Simply select the 'Blue' transport option in the RSVP form above. Dedicated electric Blue vehicles will pick up guests right outside Pipera Metro Station (Wave 1: 17:15–17:35 for the 18:00 Ceremony; Wave 2: 18:15–18:35 for the 19:00 Party) and bring you directly to Pădurile Regale. Return rides will also run continuously throughout the night to bring you safely back to Bucharest.

Leave the ski boots at home! Our dress code is 'Black Tie Optional / Forest & Alpine Chic' (elegant evening attire). Since the venue is set in the middle of a natural oak forest with wooden decks, we recommend comfortable heels/block heels or bringing dancing flats for dancing till late into the night!

Yes! Pădurile Regale features a large, secure, complimentary private parking lot right on site. You can even leave your car safely overnight if you decide to hop into one of our Blue shuttles!

The Ceremony begins at 18:00 beneath the giant oak trees at Pădurile Regale (we recommend arriving 10-15 minutes before). The Main Party starts at 19:00 and continues until early morning.
